01 / Undersea autonomyLong-Endurance Autonomous Underwater Vehicle

01 Long-endurance AUV · V4 concept

A blended hull.
A different way to travel.

The AUV is designed around a batoid-inspired blended-wing body. Buoyancy-driven glide is intended for transit; paired electric thrusters are intended for work on station. The whole planform generates lift—there are no separate aircraft-style wings attached to a torpedo hull.

Design, analysis & simulationNo hardware has been wet-tested.

Concept of operations

Glide. Work. Report.
Return to recover.

  1. 01Glide transit

    Buoyancy changes are intended to drive a sawtooth glide between areas.

  2. 02Work on station

    Paired wingtip electric thrusters are specified for powered work at the stated design speed.

  3. 03Surface & report

    The concept brings the tail and centre-body hump above the surface for position and communications.

  4. 04Return & recover

    A return-to-recovery phase is part of the mission concept; field validation remains ahead.

One hull · two defined roles

Free-swimming AUV.
Tethered ROV.

The same hull is intended for autonomous transit and survey, or for tethered close inspection from a support vessel. A tethered demonstration remains a future step, not a demonstrated capability.

Two concept roles

Different profiles.
One family.

The concepts define different roles and intended effectors; neither variant is flight-tested.

S
ROLE 01 / CAPTURE

Sprint

High speed, short endurance, vertical launch and dash for high-closure threats near an asset. The stated concept is capture by net or forced landing, designed to be recoverable and reusable.

Concept role · no validated performance figures
P
ROLE 02 / IMPACT

Picket

Lower speed, longer endurance and station-holding over a sector where launch points are sparse. The stated concept is direct kinetic interception.

Concept role · no validated performance figures

The bench article is different

Printed in-house.
Not a flight article.

The current airframe is a tail-sitter layout with four wing-tip nacelles and a nose sensor aperture. The printed structure was used for layout, structure and fit validation. Propulsion and avionics are not fitted; it has not flown.

03 / Integrated counter-UASVAJRA-KAAL · System concept

03 System architecture · design intent

VAJRA-KAAL.
Five layers, one proposed system.

VAJRA-KAAL is an indigenous, AI-native counter-UAS ecosystem. Its architecture connects sensing, AI fusion, operator command, response and a distributed network. Most layers remain design intent—not deployed capability.

Research & early prototyping · no operational capability claimed
01

Detection

Planned integration of radar, EO/IR, RF and acoustic sensors through a hardware-agnostic interface. Radar, RF and acoustic sensing are not currently built.

Planned layer
02

AI fusion & intelligence

The proposed AI Core is intended to correlate tracks, classify contacts and recommend a response. ML work is underway; multi-sensor fusion is not built.

Work in progress
03

Command

A proposed Battlefield Operating System would present a common operating picture and operator interface. No Battlefield OS build currently exists.

Not yet built
04

Engagement

Design intent is electronic countermeasures first, followed by an interceptor when required. Jamming and spoofing hardware is not built.

Concept in design
05

Distributed network

Sensor posts, launch containers and command vehicles are proposed elements. Networked nodes and launch containers are not fielded or integrated.

Future integration

Design intent is not a fielded system. No validated performance figures are stated. The layered architecture describes a proposed integration path.

Current boundary · August 2026

VAJRA-KAAL remains a system concept. Current work includes a multirotor EO-camera testbed with onboard compute; printed interceptor airframes validated for bench layout and fit; and ML development. There is no powered or flown interceptor, radar, RF or acoustic sensing, multi-sensor fusion, jamming or spoofing hardware, Battlefield OS build, networked nodes, launch container, or validated performance figures.
