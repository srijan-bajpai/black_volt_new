01 The system · design intent

One picture.
Five connected layers.

VAJRA-KAAL is documented as a layered counter-UAS architecture—not a fielded configuration. Each layer is intended to work through an AI core, with sensor and effector choices left open as indigenous systems mature.

Architecture · design intent
01

Detection

Radar, EO/IR, RF and acoustic sensing feed a hardware-agnostic sensor interface. The document intends this layer to integrate mature partner systems as they become available.

Planned layer
02

Fusion & intelligence

The proposed AI Core correlates tracks, classifies contacts, assesses threats and recommends an effector. ML development is underway; multi-sensor fusion is not currently built.

Core in development
03

Command

A proposed Battlefield Operating System would provide a common operating picture, engagement management and an operator interface. No Battlefield OS build currently exists.

Not yet built
04

Engagement

The design intent is electronic countermeasures first, followed by an interceptor when required. Jamming and spoofing hardware is not yet built; interceptor airframes are unpowered and unflown.

Concept in design
05

Network

A distributed mesh of sensor posts, launch containers and command vehicles is intended to keep operating in a degraded mode. Networked nodes are not currently fielded or integrated.

Future integration
!

These layers describe the documented architecture. They should not be read as capabilities currently available in hardware or software.

02 Layer 4 · concept in design

Not one interceptor.
A family of roles.

The PDF describes two concept roles, intended to share an autonomy build, a launch-container interface and a ground stack. The 3D forms here are original visual interpretations—not configured units, engineering drawings or tested aircraft.

Concept role / 01S

Sprint

A high-speed, short-endurance concept for vertical launch and dash against high-closure threats near an asset.

Intended effectorCapture

Net or forced-landing intercept; a recoverable, reusable concept for airspace where debris is unacceptable.

Concept role / 02P

Picket

A lower-speed, longer-endurance concept intended to hold station over a sector where launch points are sparse.

Intended effectorImpact

Direct kinetic-intercept concept for hardened, fast or fully autonomous targets.

↳ No validated speed, endurance, range or performance figures are available. The programme document states that none are quoted.
